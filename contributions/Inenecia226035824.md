I was assigned Student 2 - Budget Management Module. I developed budget.c and budget.h which allows the user to enter departmental budgets, enter expenditure, calculate the remaining budget using remaining = allocated - expenditure, determine budget status as WITHIN BUDGET or EXCEEDED, display budget information in the required format, and identify departments that have exceeded their budget. I implemented this using arrays to store up to 50 departments, loops and conditions for searching and validation, and meaningful use of C string functions strlen(), strcmp(), strcpy() and strcspn(). My functions include addDepartmentBudget(), enterExpenditure(), calculateRemainingBudget(), determineBudgetStatus(), searchBudgetByDepartment(), displayAllBudgets() and displayExceedingBudgets().
